I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://github.com/hkust-nlp), Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, advised by [Professor Junxian He](https://scholar.google.com/). My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. My research interests include LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning, hallucination in vision-language models (VLM), as well as LLM truthfulness and interpretability.

I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where I received the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship. Professor Junxian He also advised me during my undergraduate studies at SJTU.
